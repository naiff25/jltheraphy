About the Practice

A licensed professional here to guide you.

Hello, and welcome. My name is Dr. Johanna Logarzo, and I am a licensed mental health counselor in the state of Florida. I am bilingual and fluent in both English and Spanish, which allows me to connect with and support a diverse range of clients.

I earned my Doctorate in Psychology from California Southern University and my Master's degree in Mental Health Counseling from Nova Southeastern University. I have experience working with children, adolescents, adults, and older adults, helping individuals navigate a variety of challenges.

My goal is to create a safe, compassionate space where you feel heard and understood, while helping you build the tools needed to grow, heal, and reach your fullest potential.

Areas of Focus

Comprehensive care tailored to you.

I take an integrative and personalized approach to therapy, tailoring each session to meet your unique needs and goals. All services provided via secure telehealth.

Individual Therapy

Develop positive attitudes towards life's complex circumstances using cognitive behavioral, brief, and solution-focused interventions.

Family Therapy

Guided support for families working as a unit. Utilizing family systems approaches to identify issues and improve the family dynamic.

Couples Therapy

Work as a team to solve problems, change unhelpful behaviors, and build healthy communication skills and compromise in your relationship.

Child Therapy

Techniques such as art and play therapy to help children surpass difficulties like bullying, trauma, family conflicts, and self-expression.

Anger Management

Individual sessions to identify stressors and develop coping skills for constructive conflict resolution (including court-mandated clients).

Specialized Services

Providing EAP Assessments, Psychological Evaluations, Comprehensive Behavioral Assessments, and ESA (Emotional Support Animal) Letters.
